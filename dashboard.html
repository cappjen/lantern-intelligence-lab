Important limitation

What this dataset does—and does not—show

The explorer tracks selected federal cases identified through public government announcements. It reflects enforcement and publication patterns, not the total incidence or geographic prevalence of human trafficking. Allegations are labeled as such until resolved through a plea, conviction, or other court outcome.
