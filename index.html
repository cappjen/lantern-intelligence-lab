Methodology

Careful inquiry over certainty.

Lantern uses only lawful, publicly available information. Research products distinguish verified facts from analytical judgments and avoid claiming that public data provides a complete picture.

  • Source evaluation and corroboration
  • Explicit confidence assessments
  • Privacy-preserving presentation
  • Documented analytical limitations

About Lantern

Technology, analysis, and public service.

Lantern Intelligence Lab is an independent research project created to develop rigorous, ethical work at the intersection of open-source intelligence, investigative analytics, technology, and public safety. It is not a law-enforcement agency and does not conduct investigations into private individuals.
